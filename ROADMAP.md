# Roadmap

Near-term work focuses on reproducible public installation, broader source and contradiction cases,
clearer uncertainty reporting, compatibility testing across maintained OpenClaw releases, and lower
latency without weakening the evidence audit.

Potential work must be supported by a concrete failure case or evaluation. ClaimAudit will not claim
universal factual accuracy, treat source count as truth, expose private model reasoning, or execute a
research recommendation as a side effect.
