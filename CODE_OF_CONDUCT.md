# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized
conduct and sustained disruption are not accepted in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests or contributions that violate these
standards and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should consider
context, severity, prior behavior and the safety of affected people.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on the
[rin-proxy profile](https://github.com/rin-proxy). Do not disclose another person's private details
in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical.
